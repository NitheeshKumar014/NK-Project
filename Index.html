Last Soul Ultra Marathon

The last Last Soul Ultra took place from August 14–18, 2026, at a former military base near Düsseldorf, Germany. Race Details and Winner Winner: Mark Dowdle won the event as the last runner standing after completing 94 loops (approximately 395 miles or 630.4 km) in 94 hours. Runner-Up: Ayyoub “Ello” El Osrouti of Germany pushed Dowdle to the limit before stopping after 93 loops. Field: The grueling backyard-style ultramarathon began with 169 starting athletes and set a record for the longest backyard ultra held on German soil
